Dynasty Mover Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

Dynasty Mover is a fantasy football app that helps you judge trades, set lineups and find pickups. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: your account holds only your email address and your plan. Your leagues and trades stay on your phone. The app has no ads and no tracking, and your information is never sold.

Your account

To use the app you create an account with your email address and a password. We store:

  • your email address, and the date you signed up (to run the free trial)
  • your plan: free trial, subscribed, or free access given by the developer
  • whether your Google Play subscription is active, with its order number
  • the app version you use and the last time you opened the app

Accounts are run with Google Firebase. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication; the developer never sees it. The developer can see the list above to run the service and answer support requests. It is not sold, rented or shared with anyone else, and it is not used for advertising.

Payments

Subscriptions are sold through Google Play. Google handles your payment details; the app and the developer never receive your card or bank information. Google tells the app whether your subscription is active.

Information you enter

You can type your Sleeper username or a Sleeper league ID. The app sends it to Sleeper's public API to look up your leagues and download public league information: rosters, team names, trades and matchups. The username and the downloaded league information are saved only on your phone, in the app's private storage.

Your GM (the chat)

Pro has a chat called Your GM that answers questions about your league. When you ask it something, the app sends your question, the conversation so far, and a "league brief" to a server run by the developer on Cloudflare, where an AI model (Cloudflare Workers AI running Meta's Llama, or Anthropic's Claude if the developer configures it) writes the answer and streams it back. The brief holds what is already on your phone: your league's team names, rosters, records and recent trades, the app's values and grades, your projected lineup and suggested pickups. It does not include your email, your name or anything from your account.

The developer's server keeps a count of questions per account per day and nothing else; it does not store your questions or the brief. Cloudflare processes the request under its privacy policy; if Anthropic's model is used, Anthropic processes it under its privacy policy and does not use API data to train its models. The conversation itself is saved only on your phone, and "Clear chat" in the GM screen removes it.

Importing your team from a screenshot

If you choose "Upload your team from a screenshot", the app shrinks the picture you pick and sends it to the developer's server on Cloudflare, where an AI model (Cloudflare Workers AI's vision model) reads the player names, positions and teams in it and sends that list back. The picture is used only to read your roster: the developer's server does not store it, share it or use it for anything else, and it counts toward the same daily limit as a question to Your GM. The players it finds are saved only on your phone, as your team. Cloudflare processes the request under its privacy policy.

Information the app does not collect

The app does not collect your name, contacts, location, advertising ID or device identifiers, and it keeps no photos (a screenshot you choose to import is read and discarded, above). It has no ads, analytics or tracking. Your leagues, rosters, trades and settings are not sent to the developer, except as part of a question you ask Your GM or a screenshot you import (above).

Services the app contacts

To download player values and league information, the app connects directly from your phone to these services. Like any website, each one receives your IP address and basic request details, and handles them under its own privacy policy.

The app sends these services only what a request needs, such as a league ID, your league's format or a stadium's location. It sends nothing else about you.

Deleting your information

Your account: in the app, open the League tab and tap Delete my account. This deletes your sign-in and your account record right away. You can also email the address below from your account's email and ask for deletion; it is done within 30 days. Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription; cancel it in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions).

Your leagues: go to the League tab and tap × next to a league. To delete everything on the phone, uninstall the app or clear its storage in your phone's settings.

Records of purchases may be kept by Google as required for tax and accounting.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
